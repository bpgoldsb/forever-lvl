* **Product Nature & Publisher:** *World of Warcraft: Forever* is an official Blizzard Entertainment standalone title, not an emulation project, mod, or private server. ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com)) — `[confirmed]`

## Step 2: Verification and Refutation of Specific Claims

### 1. Curse of Agony / Bane System & Direct Damage
* **Claim:** *Curse of Agony renamed "Bane of Agony"; banes use a separate aura slot from curses; Bane of Agony does direct damage on cast.*
* **Status:** **Partially Confirmed & Partially Refuted**.
  * *Rename to Bane of Agony:* Curse of Agony was renamed **Bane of Agony** (along with Curse of Doom becoming Bane of Doom). ([icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com), [blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Separate Aura Slot:* Banes are no longer categorized as Curses, allowing Warlocks to maintain both a Bane (e.g., Bane of Agony) and a utility Curse (e.g., Curse of the Elements, Curse of Recklessness) simultaneously on the same target. ([icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com), [mmoexp.com](https://www.mmoexp.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Direct Damage on Cast:* **Refuted**. Bane of Agony remains an exclusive damage-over-time (DoT) effect with a ramp-up damage mechanic and does **not** deal direct/initial damage on cast. Spells receiving upfront initial damage mechanics in *Forever* include *Immolate* via talents like *Aftermath*, but not Bane of Agony. ([boosthive.eu](https://boosthive.eu), [mobalytics.gg](https://mobalytics.gg)) — `[confirmed]`

### 2. DoT Crits & "Pandemic" Talent
* **Claim:** *DoT ticks (Corruption, Bane of Agony) can crit via a "Pandemic" talent.*
* **Status:** **Partially Confirmed / Clarified**.
  * *DoT Periodic Crits:* Under the modernized client fork of *Forever*, periodic damage ticks can critically strike inherently; the ability to crit is not granted by the talent itself. ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [mobalytics.gg](https://mobalytics.gg)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Pandemic Talent:* A new 3-rank talent located in Row 3 of the Affliction tree. It increases the **critical strike damage bonus** of periodic spells (Corruption, Bane of Agony, Bane of Doom, Drain Soul, Drain Life, Siphon Life, Wrack) by 33% per rank, up to a maximum of **+100% bonus critical damage** at 3/3. It amplifies critical strike magnitude, while critical strike chance is governed by stats and separate talents (such as *Malevolence*). ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [conquestcapped.com](https://conquestcapped.com)) — `[confirmed]`

### 3. Life Tap Spirit Scaling & "Soul Harvesting"
* **Claim:** *Life Tap scales with Spirit; "Soul Harvesting" gives mana regen on Drain Soul kills.*
* **Status:** **Confirmed**.
  * *Life Tap Spirit Scaling:* Life Tap has been reworked to convert Health into Mana based on base values plus a percentage scaling directly with the Warlock's **Spirit** attribute rather than Spell Power, revitalizing Spirit as an active stat on Warlock gear. ([bindonforever.com](https://bindonforever.com), [icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com), [blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Soul Harvesting Talent:* A new Affliction talent that replaces Classic's *Improved Drain Soul*. When an enemy dies during Drain Soul, 50–100% of normal mana regeneration continues while casting, and total mana regeneration is increased by 50–100% for 10 seconds. ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com)) — `[confirmed]`

### 4. Demon Scaling & Drain Soul Shard Generation
* **Claim:** *Pets inherit master spell/stat scaling; Drain Soul ticks can generate a Soul Shard before the mob dies.*
* **Status:** **Confirmed**.
  * *Pet Stat Inheritance:* Demons inherit dynamic attributes from their master: Warlock demons receive a direct percentage of the master's **Spell Power, Stamina, and Resistances**. ([mobalytics.gg](https://mobalytics.gg), [blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Drain Soul Mid-Channel Shards:* Drain Soul generates Soul Shards periodically during channel ticks prior to the target's death, in addition to guaranteeing a shard upon a killing blow. ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com)) — `[confirmed]`

### 5. Orc Racials: Blood Fury & Shatter Curse
* **Claim:** *Orc Blood Fury: +10% spell power for 15s, no healing debuff; a racial "Shatter Curse": 8s curse/bane immunity plus reduced magic damage.*
* **Status:** **Confirmed**.
  * *Blood Fury (Active):* Increases Attack Power and Spell Power by **10% for 15 seconds** with **no healing reduction penalty** applied to the Orc. ([mobalytics.gg](https://mobalytics.gg), [warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Shatter Curse (Active):* An Orc racial granting **immunity to Curses and Banes** and reducing incoming **magical damage by 15% for 8 seconds**. ([lfcarry.com](https://lfcarry.com), [blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com)) — `[confirmed]`

### 6. Camping & Cooking Kit System
* **Claim:** *Campfire tiers Basic (3 items, Cooking 1), Journeyman (5, Cooking 140), Expert (10, Cooking 220); sit 1 min for a 1-hour buff; 1 fixture per player per hour. Fixtures: Fish Bowl (Fishing 20, +8% all stats), Fishing Rack (140), Fishing Hut (300); Camp Chair (Skinning 20, +2% spell/melee crit), Field Guide (140), Trapper's Workbench (300); Incense Candle (Herbalism 20, intellect buff), Greenhouse (140), Seed Hybridizer (300); Mana Well (Alchemy 20, MP5); Cookie's Feast (Cooking 140).*
* **Status:** **Confirmed**.
  * *Campfire Tiers:* Crafted via Cooking kits:
    * **Basic Campfire:** Cooking 1 — 3 fixture capacity slots. ([method.gg](https://www.method.gg), [blizzardwatch.com](https://blizzardwatch.com)) — `[confirmed]`
    * **Journeyman Campfire:** Cooking 140 — 5 fixture capacity slots (Blueprint dropped by Lord Serpentis in Wailing Caverns). ([mobalytics.gg](https://mobalytics.gg), [icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com)) — `[confirmed]`
    * **Expert Campfire:** Cooking 220 — 10 fixture capacity slots. ([method.gg](https://www.method.gg), [expcarry.com](https://expcarry.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Mechanics & Limits:* Players receive 1-hour buffs by sitting (`/sit`) or crafting near the campfire for at least 1 minute. Each player may place 1 fixture per hour (3600-second shared placement cooldown). ([blizzardwatch.com](https://blizzardwatch.com), [warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Fixtures & Thresholds:*
    * **Fishing:** *Fish Bowl* (Fishing 20, +8% all stats, exclusive with Blessing of Kings); *Fishing Rack* (Fishing 140, lures + uncommon fish); *Fishing Hut* (Fishing 300, rare fish access). ([blizzardwatch.com](https://blizzardwatch.com), [method.gg](https://www.method.gg)) — `[confirmed]`
    * **Skinning:** *Camp Chair* (Skinning 20, +2% spell/melee critical strike chance); *Field Guide* (Skinning 140, Track Beasts for 1 hour); *Trapper's Workbench* (Skinning 300, provides trap & advanced crafting station). ([blizzardwatch.com](https://blizzardwatch.com), [method.gg](https://www.method.gg)) — `[confirmed]`
    * **Herbalism:** *Incense Candle* (Herbalism 20, +2 to +25 Intellect buff, exclusive with Arcane Intellect); *Greenhouse* (Herbalism 140, seed planting/herb growth); *Seed Hybridizer* (Herbalism 300, seed multiplying/upgrades). ([blizzardwatch.com](https://blizzardwatch.com), [method.gg](https://www.method.gg)) — `[confirmed]`
    * **Alchemy:** *Mana Well* (Alchemy 20, passive mana regen / MP5, exclusive with Blessing of Wisdom). ([method.gg](https://www.method.gg)) — `[confirmed]`
    * **Cooking:** *Cookie's Feast* (Cooking 140, provides party/raid Stamina food buff). ([method.gg](https://www.method.gg)) — `[confirmed]`

### 6. Earliest Obtainable Wands (Levels 1–20)

Wands provide uninterrupted caster DPS without mana consumption, forming the backbone of Warlock leveling efficiency alongside DoTs.

* **Vendor Wands:**
  * Neither Durotar nor The Barrens features general or weapon vendors that sell early wands. White/green crafted wands sell to vendors for 1c in *Forever*, but must be acquired from crafters or the Auction House. ([bluetracker.gg](https://bluetracker.gg), [warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com)) — `[confirmed]`
* **Crafted Wands (Enchanting):**
  1. **Novice's Practice Wand:**
     * *Level Requirement:* Level 1 to equip.
     * *Source:* Crafted at **Enchanting Skill 1** (introduced in *WoW Forever* to solve caster early weapon drought). ([foreverdb.net](https://foreverdb.net), [warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  2. **Lesser Magic Wand:**
     * *Level Requirement:* Level 5 to equip.
     * *DPS / Stats:* ~11.3 DPS (Arcane Damage); includes Spell Power in *Forever*.
     * *Source:* Crafted at **Enchanting Skill 10** using 1x *Simple Wood* (vendor) and 1x *Lesser Magic Essence* (disenchanting green items). Available early on the Orgrimmar Auction House for 5–10s. ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com)) — `[confirmed]`
  3. **Greater Magic Wand:**
     * *Level Requirement:* Level 13 to equip.
     * *DPS / Stats:* ~17.5 DPS; includes Spell Power bonus.
     * *Source:* Crafted at **Enchanting Skill 70** using 1x *Simple Wood* and 1x *Greater Magic Essence*. Available on the Orgrimmar Auction House. ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com)) — `[confirmed]`
* **Quest Wands (Horde 1–20):**
  * *Durotar & The Barrens:* **None**. There are **no quest reward wands** anywhere in Durotar or The Barrens during the level 1–20 range. (Unlike Alliance who receive wands in Darkshore/Westfall, Horde casters have zero early quest wands). ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/wow)) — `[confirmed]`
  * *Earliest Horde Quest Wand:* **Gravestone Scepter** (29.0 DPS), rewarded from the dungeon quest *"Blackfathom Villainy"* in Blackfathom Deeps (Ashenvale border, recommended levels 18–24). ([classicdb.ch](https://classicdb.ch)) — `[inferred]`
* **Practical Acquisition Strategy:**
  * Relying on quests for wands will leave an Orc Warlock wandless throughout Durotar and The Barrens.
  * Train Skinning and Herbalism, vendor light leather scraps and early herbs to generate ~7–12 silver by level 5–7, then visit the **Orgrimmar Auction House** to buy a crafted **Lesser Magic Wand** (level 5), followed by a **Greater Magic Wand** at level 13. ([blizzard.com](https://news.blizzard.com), [method.gg](https://method.gg)) — `[confirmed]`
