## 1. Warlock spell levels 1-7 -- confirmed (guide, not DB)
Source: https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-warlock-leveling-guide-and-talents
- L1: Shadow Bolt, Demon Skin, Immolate ("learn Immolate from the trainer for 10 copper"), Summon Imp (L1 quest).
- L4: Corruption and Curse of Weakness (trained together). Grimoire of Blood Pact picked up at L4 from a Demon Trainer.
- L6: Life Tap (rank 1) and Shadow Bolt Rank 2. This settles Life Tap = 6, not 16.
- Later: L8 Bane of Agony; L10 Drain Soul, Create Healthstone, Summon Voidwalker; L14 Drain Life; L18 Create Soulstone, Searing Pain; L20 Demon Armor, Rain of Fire, Ritual of Summoning.
- Corroboration (search snippet of a Sep 2026 beta guide, URL not opened): levels 4, 6, 8, 10, 20 essential (Corruption, Life Tap, Bane of Agony, Drain Soul/Healthstone, Ritual of Summoning).
Caveat: guide text, not the Wowhead Forever DB; ranks other than Shadow Bolt R2 not given.

## 6. Wands -- confirmed
Source: https://seemeta.com/en/wow-forever/professions/enchanting
- Novice's Practice Wand: Enchanting trainer recipe, skill 10/13/15 (yellow/green/grey), reagents Simple Wood + Mote of Magic. Craftable at Enchanting 1 (per search snippet of a Forever guide).
- Lesser Magic Wand: trainer recipe, 75/95/115, Simple Wood + Lesser Magic Essence.
- Greater Magic Wand: trainer recipe, 110/130/150.
- Required item levels of Lesser/Greater Magic Wand: not found for Forever (Classic: Greater = L13, unverified here).
- Vendor-sold wand in Durotar/Orgrimmar: not found. method.gg: no BoP enchanter wands early.
- Apprentice Enchanting: no level requirement (search snippet, https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/enchanting-leveling-guide not opened).

- Herbalism (https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/herbalism-trainers): Zor'la, Valley of Trials (junior, to 75); Mishiki, Sen'jin Village; Jandi, The Drag, Orgrimmar (full). None in Barrens. No level requirement; Journeyman skill 50.
- Skinning (https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/skinning-trainers): Kagil, The Den, Durotar (junior to 75); Thuwd, The Drag, Orgrimmar (full); Dranh, Camp Taurajo, Barrens (full). No level requirement; Journeyman skill 50.
- Fishing (https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/fishing-trainers): Lau'Tiki, Durotar (sub-location not given); Lumak, Valley of Honor, Orgrimmar. None in Barrens. Apprentice no level requirement; Journeyman needs L10 + Fishing 50.
- Cooking (https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/cooking-trainers): Cook Torka, Razor Hill; Zamja, The Drag, Orgrimmar; Duhng, Barrens just east of Crossroads (inside the tower). Apprentice no level requirement.
- Minimum character level to learn Apprentice professions: none (Herbalism, Skinning, Fishing, Cooking, Enchanting pages).
