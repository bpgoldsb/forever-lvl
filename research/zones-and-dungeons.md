## Main result

**No page gives a level range for an old zone in Forever.** `agy` tagged 21 zone ranges (Elwynn 1-10, Westfall 10-20, The Barrens 10-25, Duskwood 18-25, and so on) as confirmed by the three foreverwisp.com pages. Those pages hold no level range for any zone. The 1-20 page names the zones each race's route passes through, in order; the two 20-60 pages name no zone at all. The numbers `agy` gave are Classic's.

So the only Forever level ranges for zones are the three Blizzard stated for new zones, and the only other Forever ranges are for dungeons.

## Stated by agy, not on the page or not checked

- Every level range for an old zone (see Main result). Classic's ranges, as `agy` listed them from the wiki's Classic page, are in the raw answer; they are `inferred` at best and the page was not re-read.
- Zone order for levels 20-35 (Duskwood, Wetlands, Hillsbrad, Ashenvale, Stranglethorn or Desolace for the Alliance; the Barrens, Stonetalon, Hillsbrad, Ashenvale, Thousand Needles for the Horde) and every town named with it (Southshore, Tarren Mill, Nijel's Point, Freewind Post and others). The two 20-60 pages name none of these; the route itself is behind a purchase.
- Dwarf and Gnome going on to Westfall, Redridge and Duskwood, and Orc, Troll and Tauren towns by name. The route page does not say.
- Alliance Skyborne leaving by airship to Dalaran and Horde Skyborne to a new Skywatcher Plateau in Mulgore: wiki only. The datamine page confirms only a Skyborne "Portal to Dalaran".
- Level ranges for the 20 old dungeons above the beta cap (Blackfathom Deeps 25-31 through Stratholme 58-60), and a second set of ranges for the five old dungeons in the table (Ragefire 13-19, Deadmines 18-24, Wailing Caverns 19-25, Shadowfang 23-29, Stockade 26-32): wiki only. The rows use the fan site's client numbers.
- Krol'dok Stronghold at 50-55, City of Dalaran in Arathi Highlands, The Drowned City in Stranglethorn Vale, Blackmaw Hold in Azshara, Barrow Deeps inside Mount Hyjal: wiki only, and each disagrees with the fan site.
- "Old dungeons retuned": the dungeon page lists ranges and does not say they changed.
- Skyborne classes without Rogue: `agy` took the datamine page's list of client templates. Blizzard's recap includes Rogue.
- Zone changes hung on the wiki's Riverglades page (new passes from Redridge, Burning Steppes, Badlands and Swamp of Sorrows; a boat from Powderfuse Port to Steamwheedle Port in Tanaris).

## Zone list check, 2026-10-12 (part 1 of "zones through 60")

Claude, by curl, no `agy` run. https://warcraftforever.games/maps (saved as `fw-maps-index.*`, plus `fw-map-<zone>.html` for Riverglades, Shen'dralas, Mount Hyjal and Darkspear Islands, and `fw-home-20261012.html`) lists the beta client's zones: 26 Eastern Kingdoms, 23 Kalimdor, with Riverglades, Mount Hyjal and Shen'dralas marked new, and Darkspear Islands and Zephras Isle under "Battlegrounds & islands". No per-zone page, and no list, states a level range for an old zone; the map pages hold NPC levels only, which are not a zone range. The only Forever ranges are still Zephras Isle 1-12, Riverglades "mid-30s to mid-40s" and Mount Hyjal 60 (the home page).
- Darkspear Islands is a battleground, not a leveling zone. Ruins of Gilneas is not on the maps page. Bandarion Keep is a town in Tirisfal Glades (the trainer list says so). None is a zone entry.
- Stonetalon Mountains is not in the `agy` table; its Classic range (15-27) and faction are from memory, `inferred`, not re-read.
